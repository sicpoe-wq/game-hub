# Music and sound effects

All music and sound effects under `app/src/main/res/raw/` were originally composed for Heartbound in Tim's Game Hub. They were written as short procedural loops and cues (see `tools/heartbound-audio.py`) and then encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

They are released under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may copy, modify, and redistribute them with no attribution required, though a credit is appreciated.

Tracks:

- `mus_hall` — morning and hall theme
- `mus_evening` — tavern and evening theme
- `mus_battle` — combat theme
- `mus_romance` — confession, date, and visit theme
- `mus_ending` — ending theme
- `mus_title` — title and opening-animation theme
- `sfx_tap`, `sfx_hit`, `sfx_heal`, `sfx_level`, `sfx_gift`, `sfx_ach` — interface and battle cues
