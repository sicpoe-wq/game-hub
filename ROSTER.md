# Political piece sets (as of 17 November 2026)

Game Hub Chess ships two “Democrats vs Republicans” piece sets that share this roster. White is Republicans (red accent); Black is Democrats (blue accent). Office-holders were checked against contemporaneous coverage on 17 November 2026, during the lame-duck session of the 119th Congress. Democrats won the 2026 midterms and will control the House and Senate starting 3 January 2027; titles below say “sitting” vs “designate / elect” accordingly.

People who left office, retired, lost, or died in 2026 are **not** in the set (among them Lindsey Graham, Mitch McConnell as a continuing member, Nancy Pelosi, Elise Stefanik, Lauren Boebert, Chip Roy, Jasmine Crockett, and Kamala Harris).

## King and queen choices

- **Republican king — Donald J. Trump**, the sitting President of the United States (sworn in 20 January 2025).
- **Republican queen — Melania Trump**, the sitting First Lady.
- **Democratic king — Hakeem Jeffries**, House Democratic Leader and Speaker-designate. After the midterm wave he is the most prominent national Democratic officeholder and the incoming holder of the highest constitutional office of any Democrat (the Speaker is second in the presidential line of succession). The formal Speakership vote is 3 January 2027; the House Democratic leadership meeting was scheduled for 18 November 2026. **Alternative considered:** Chuck Schumer, the sitting Senate Democratic Leader (caucus vote vs. Chris Van Hollen scheduled 1 December 2026). Jeffries was chosen because the Speakership is the higher office once the new Congress convenes.
- **Democratic queen — Katherine Clark**, House Democratic Whip and widely expected first woman House Majority Leader. The brief allows “spouse of the party’s leader or a top female leader.” Clark (a public official and the top female elected Democratic leader) was chosen rather than Jeffries’ spouse, Kennisandra Arciniegas-Jeffries, who is a private citizen / social worker.

## Public vs private art

- **Political (Parody)** ships in the public build: even-handed cartoon / bobblehead caricatures, with the in-app note “Parody — not affiliated with or endorsed by anyone depicted.”
- **Political (Likeness)** is a private, more lifelike character-art set for Tim’s own APK only. Those assets live in `app/src/private/` (gitignored) and are compiled only with `./gradlew assembleDebug -Plikeness=true`. They are not in the public GitHub repository or the public `v2.1-debug` release.

Build the public APK with `./gradlew assembleDebug`. Build the private APK with `./gradlew assembleDebug -Plikeness=true` (application id suffix `.private`).
