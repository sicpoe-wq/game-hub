# Game Hub

Android game hub (Kotlin + Jetpack Compose).

Playable games:
- **Word Search** — themed letter grids, three difficulties
- **Water Sort** — pour colors between tubes, endless levels
- **Chess** — full legal rules (castling, en passant, promotion, checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, 50-move rule, insufficient material). Play vs an offline engine (Easy / Medium / Hard) or local pass-and-play. Tap or drag pieces, undo, move list, captured pieces, optional clock, resign/draw, and save/resume. A Themes screen previews independent board themes and piece sets (Classic Wood, Marble, Tournament Green, Neon Cyber, Enchanted, Sakura, Lumenreach boards; Classic Staunton, Minimal Flat, Neon Glow, Fantasy, Anime Chibi, and Political (Parody) pieces). v2.1 adds a perspective 3D board mode (tilt, rotate, and pinch-zoom, with a reset-camera button; toggle 2D/3D on the board and in Themes) implemented as software 2.5D billboards in Compose Canvas, plus a public Political (Parody) Democrats vs Republicans piece set of even-handed bobblehead caricatures with an in-app parody disclaimer. v2.2 replaces 3D-mode billboards with a real OpenGL ES 2.0 board: procedural lathe Staunton meshes, political figures as camera-facing curved cards on a shared 3D pedestal, textured wood/marble/metal boards with a beveled engraved frame, themed backdrops, arc-glide moves, capture particles, check pulse, checkmate camera sweep, idle motion, a promotion effect, MSAA, an opening camera fly-in, and a High quality graphics toggle (off is a lighter path for weaker phones). See `ROSTER.md` for the November 2026 roster and sources. A private Political (Likeness) set is not in this repository; it is compiled only locally with `./gradlew assembleDebug -Plikeness=true`. v2.3 is a private-build visual pass for that likeness set (lifelike figures with chess-piece costumes, cleaned cutouts); there is no public v2.3 release of those assets. Subtle board animations (neon pulse, petals, sparkles, lanterns) can be toggled in Settings, along with coordinates and original CC0 sound effects.
- **Heartbound** — run an adventurer's guild in Lumenreach, and maybe fall for your heroes. v1.9 adds a skippable opening animation (night sky, lanterns, parallax, hero silhouettes, and a glowing HEARTBOUND logo) synced to an original title theme, plus an animated title menu with Continue, Gallery, Settings, and a version label. A Play intro setting turns the animation on or off. v1.8 adds full-screen CG art for confessions, vows, endings, and key plot beats, a gallery that unlocks as those moments are seen, original looping music (hall, evening, battle, romance, ending) and sound effects, and music/SFX volume sliders plus mute in Settings. Audio is original and released under CC0 (see `LICENSE-AUDIO.md`). v1.7 added hall life: decorate the hall and hero rooms, private room visits, forge/library/garden/stables, hireable staff, crafting and enchanting, a traveling merchant with seasonal stock, seasons and weather, hero birthdays, a town map with date locations, a secret late hero (Nim Hale) with her own route, guild endings, and quietly animated portraits. v1.6 added turn-based battles, expeditions, bosses, branching chains, Iron Vesper, bond combos, and reputation. v1.5 added exclusive routes, group events, chapter 1, and fade-to-black cutaways, plus the v1.4 creation and QoL features.
